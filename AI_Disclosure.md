# AI Disclosure

I believe in being transparent about how these projects are developed.

These No Man's Sky utilities began as personal projects. I wrote the original Python code and subsequently used ChatGPT extensively to help refine, expand, debug, and improve their functionality.

For projects that include HTML-based interfaces, I relied more heavily on ChatGPT. My HTML skills are admittedly rusty, and AI assistance allowed me to implement interfaces and functionality that would otherwise have taken considerably more time.

My involvement includes:

* **Core development:** I created the original tools, identified the problems they needed to solve, and continue to direct their functionality, design, and development.

* **AI-assisted development:** I use ChatGPT to help implement enhancements, troubleshoot issues, refactor code, and explore technical solutions.

* **Documentation:** I write the original documentation and use ChatGPT to help organize, refine, and standardize it according to my preferences.

* **Testing:** I use ChatGPT to create and execute test scripts, review results, and help identify potential problems.

* **Build and distribution:** I reuse and adapt build scripts from my other projects, with ChatGPT assisting in updating project-specific paths, filenames, and packaging configurations.

### A Word About AI-Assisted Development

I am not a professional software developer, nor do I claim to be one. These are tools I originally built to make my own No Man's Sky experience easier and more enjoyable.

Over time, I realized that other players might find them useful, too. Rather than keep them to myself, I decided to share them freely with the community.

AI has helped me turn personal utilities into more capable, accessible, and maintainable tools. I make no apologies for using technology that helps me accomplish those goals.

You're welcome to examine the code, suggest improvements, report bugs, contribute, or simply use the tools as they are.

You're equally welcome to decide that AI-assisted software isn't for you.

**What I'm not interested in is debating the legitimacy of AI-assisted development.** You're entitled to your opinions about AI, just as I'm entitled to use it.

Ultimately, these projects exist because I enjoy creating useful things and sharing them with fellow players.

That's really all there is to it.
